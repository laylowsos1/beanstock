# Trading Strategy

## Mission
Use a disciplined, catalyst-aware swing-trading process to grow a small account while controlling downside. Paper/simulated mode first.

## Capital & Contributions
- Starting capital target: $300
- Planned contribution: $300 every two weeks
- Small-account mode until equity is large enough to diversify without overconcentration

## Instruments
- Stocks / eligible fractional shares first
- No options initially
- No margin
- No short selling

## Portfolio Rules
1. Max 5-6 open positions.
2. Initial position size while equity < $2,000: target 8-12%, hard cap 15%.
3. Absolute max exposure to one company: 20%.
4. Max sector exposure: 30%.
5. Max 3 new trades per week.
6. Maintain cash when no high-quality setup exists.
7. Do not force 75-85% deployment in a tiny account; deployment is opportunity-dependent.

## Entry Gate
Every proposed buy must pass:
- Specific catalyst or durable thesis documented today.
- Candidate score >= 75; prefer >= 85.
- Sector trend is not clearly hostile to the thesis.
- Liquidity/spread is acceptable.
- Position fits account cash and concentration limits.
- Planned downside is defined.
- Planned reward:risk is at least 2:1 for swing trades.
- No duplicate exposure that pushes sector concentration over limits.

## Exit / Risk Rules
- Default hard thesis review around -7% from entry.
- Exit earlier if catalyst/thesis is invalidated.
- Never average down solely because price declined.
- Never widen risk after entry simply to avoid realizing a loss.
- When a winner is materially extended, protect gains rather than turning it into a loser.
- At +15%: review whether a ~7% trailing protection is technically sensible.
- At +20%: review whether a ~5% trailing protection is technically sensible.
- Do not tighten protection to an arbitrary level inside normal volatility; use technical structure and broker-supported order types.
- After two consecutive failed trades in one sector, pause new entries in that sector until weekly review.

## Candidate Score (100)
- Fundamentals 20
- Growth 15
- Valuation 15
- Technical setup 15
- Catalyst strength/timing 20
- Risk/reward 10
- Market/sector context 5

## Research Preferences
Look for:
- Beaten-down quality names with a real rerating catalyst
- Momentum names supported by fundamentals and identifiable catalysts
- Earnings/guidance resets where expectations have become asymmetric
- Product/contract/regulatory/industry catalysts
- Strong sector momentum
- Reasonable valuation versus growth and peers

Avoid:
- Social-media-only pumps
- Illiquid names with poor spreads
- Binary events where downside cannot be sensibly bounded
- Overlapping positions that create hidden concentration

## Benchmark
Track portfolio performance versus the S&P 500, but prioritize controlled drawdown and process quality over short-term outperformance.
