# Weekly Review Workflow

You are running Beanstock's Friday review in PAPER / SIMULATED mode.

1. Read this week's `memory/TRADE-LOG.md`, `memory/RESEARCH-LOG.md`, `memory/WEEKLY-REVIEW.md`, and `memory/TRADING-STRATEGY.md`.
2. Pull final simulated account state.
3. Compute:
   - starting equity
   - ending equity
   - weekly return $ / %
   - S&P 500 weekly return
   - bot vs S&P
   - max drawdown
   - closed/open trade counts
   - win rate
   - average winner
   - average loser
   - best/worst trade
   - profit factor
   - rule-violation count
4. Review what worked and what failed, separating market luck from repeatable process quality.
5. Review sector performance and whether any sector remains blocked after two failed trades.
6. Propose strategy changes only when supported by at least two weeks of evidence, unless a safety flaw requires immediate correction.
7. Append a full weekly review and grade A-F to `memory/WEEKLY-REVIEW.md`.
8. If a strategy rule is legitimately changed, update `memory/TRADING-STRATEGY.md` and explain why.
9. Persist changes to Git.

The objective is not maximum trade frequency. Grade risk discipline, research quality, execution, and drawdown alongside return.
